Specialized chemical blends, imported OEM rig spares, or custom formulations have structured lead times ranging from 5 to 14 business days, backed by real-time logistics tracking and expediting reports.

JTEX Services Limited is a fully incorporated Nigerian enterprise (RC: 1965120) fully aligned with NUPRC (Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission) environmental guidelines and NOGIC / NCDMB Local Content requirements. We maintain strict QA/QC protocols and adhere to ISO-aligned safety and quality operational benchmarks.

Every chemical batch supplied by JTEX Services is accompanied by a verified Certificate of Analysis (COA), comprehensive Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS/SDS), and batch quality test documentation ensuring exact chemical formulation compliance with operator specifications and wellbore safety parameters.

We provide a 24/7 Rapid Response Hot-Shot Dispatch Service for active drilling operations facing fluid loss, mud weight adjustments, or urgent mechanical component needs. Our operations desk coordinates expedited marshaling from regional bonded yards directly to jetty heads or onshore well sites.

Our technical team collaborates with chemical engineers and mud technicians to custom-blend viscosifiers, shale inhibitors, fluid loss control agents, and scale inhibitors tailored to specific HPHT (High Pressure High Temperature) reservoir lithologies and water-based or synthetic oil-based mud systems.

Our environmental services team follows zero-discharge and eco-remediation standards. All industrial washdowns, chemical drum disposal, and drill cuttings handling are carried out using approved bio-neutralizers, containment berms, and certified safe haulage to authorized treatment facilities with full chain-of-custody documentation.
