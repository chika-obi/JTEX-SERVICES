About JTEX Services

Trusted partner for energy & industrial procurement

Registered in Nigeria (RC: 1965120) and headquartered in Port Harcourt, JTEX Services Limited specializes in procurement and supply for the chemical and oil & gas sectors. With strong logistics and safety-first operations, we support drilling contractors, manufacturers, and industrial facilities across West Africa.
